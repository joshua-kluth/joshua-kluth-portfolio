## 📊 What Makes a Strong Portfolio

### Good Projects Have:

### Strong About Section Has:
- Personality beyond just technical skills

### Effective Skills Section Has:

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too Much Text** - Keep it scannable with bullet points
2. **No Visuals** - Add images, diagrams, charts
3. **Generic Descriptions** - Be specific about your contributions
4. **Outdated Content** - Update regularly
5. **Broken Links** - Test everything before sharing
6. **Poor Mobile Experience** - Always test on phone
7. **No Call-to-Action** - Make it easy to contact you
8. **Humble Bragging** - Be confident but genuine
9. **Typos** - Proofread multiple times
10. **Cluttered Design** - Less is more

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Maximum Impact:
1. **Lead with best work** - Put strongest projects first
2. **Use numbers** - "Improved efficiency by 30%" is better than "Made it faster"
3. **Show process** - Employers want to see how you think
4. **Update constantly** - Fresh content shows you're active
5. **Get feedback** - Ask professors, peers, mentors
6. **Mobile test** - 50%+ of viewers will be on mobile
7. **SEO basics** - Use descriptive page titles
8. **Link to code** - GitHub repos for software projects
9. **Tell stories** - Make projects interesting, not just technical
10. **Be yourself** - Authenticity stands out

### For Job Search:
1. Include portfolio link in every application
2. Reference specific projects in cover letters
3. Use portfolio as interview talking points
4. Share on LinkedIn when you add new projects
5. Ask connections to review and provide feedback

## 🎓 For Students Specifically

### Highlight:

- Clear communication of complex concepts
